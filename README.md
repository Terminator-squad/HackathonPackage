# Study Focus Tool

A web-based productivity tool built during a hackathon to help students stay focused and productive while studying. The brief was to design a tool that improves focus by structuring study time and reducing distractions, with at least one interactive feature.

## The Problem

Students often struggle to stay focused while studying — distractions, tab-switching, and a lack of structure make it hard to maintain concentration over a study session.

## Our Solution

A single website with three linked pages, each tackling a different part of the focus problem:

### Shared Study Timer
A synchronised study timer designed to be used by multiple students connected at the same time. The key feature: if any user leaves the timer page, the timer resets for everyone. This discourages tab-switching and encourages the whole group to stay locked in together, turning focus into a shared, accountable effort.

### Task List & Random Task Picker
Students can enter tasks into a list and remove them once done. To take the friction out of deciding what to work on, a random task picker selects a task for you from your list — removing decision paralysis and helping you just get started.

### Third Feature Page
A third linked page contributed by another team member, integrated into the same site.
